## Contradictions

1. **Where the ankle problem started (Villarreal, 22 Nov).** Barça Blaugranes, Yahoo, 365Scores and Jornada Perfecta say the discomfort began **in the warm-up**. The summary of SI México and others says the discomfort was suffered **in the match** ("sufridas en el partido").
2. **How serious the ankle is.** The club wording, as reported, is **"leves molestias"** (slight discomfort) and a **precaution**, with a return expected for Deportivo. Headlines go further: Récord MX has "vuelve a ser baja **por lesión**" (out again **through injury**), Agencia Nova "sufrió **una lesión** en el tobillo" (suffered **an injury** to the ankle), Hipócrita Lector "**lesión** en el tobillo". EverythingBarca goes the other way and calls missing Baku "actually good news" (rest).
   - https://www.record.com.mx/historia/lamine-yamal-vuelve-a-ser-baja-por-lesion-y-se-pierde-un-partido-clave-con-el-barcelona-2026112314101397681
   - https://www.agencianova.com/deportes-en-el-mundo/2026/11/23/175653/lamine-yamal-sufrio-una-lesion-en-el-tobillo-y-se-perdera-el-partido-del-barcelona-por-champions-league/
   - https://everythingbarca.com/lamine-yamal-injury-sabah-fk-barcelona-champions-league-rest
3. **The knee in November.** De la Fuente describes inflammation and fluid on the knee but says it is "progressing well", and he named Yamal as a starter for England. Barcelona-leaning coverage (VAVEL, El Diario Alerta) frames it as Barça protecting him from a relapse, with friction between club and national team. De la Fuente's "no privileges" line answers criticism that he was treated differently.
4. **The Atlético game story.** Al Jazeera and Foot-Africa have a 1-2 win with Fermín scoring twice. An El Universal (MX) piece found in the same search describes Atlético 2-0 up and a late Yamal winner. That is almost certainly a different season's fixture. Do not use it.
5. **The Flick penalty quote.** Wording varies by outlet ("Maybe I have to think to change it, I am not sure" against "Maybe I have to change it"). Find the original-language transcript, from the club, RAC1 or MD, before quoting.
6. **Old and new injuries mixed together.** Many search hits blend his 2024 right-ankle injury (grade one syndesmosis, picked up against Red Star) and 2025 episodes with the 2026 ones. Do not let older severity language bleed into this season's story.
